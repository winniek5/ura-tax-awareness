Understanding Taxes in Uganda

Your contribution. Our development.

Why Paying Taxes Matters

Taxes are an important source of government revenue. They help fund public services such as education, healthcare, roads and other infrastructure. By paying taxes, individuals and businesses contribute to Uganda's development and the well-being of society.

Whether you are employed or running a business, understanding your tax responsibilities helps you participate in the country's development. Paying the correct taxes also helps support essential services used by everyone.

Steps to Register for a TIN

A Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is used by URA to identify taxpayers.

  1. Visit the official URA website.
  2. Select the "Get a TIN" option.
  3. Choose the appropriate registration option.
  4. Provide the required identification and personal details.
  5. Submit your application and follow URA's instructions.

Key Tax Filing Deadlines

Tax Type Filing Deadline
VAT 15th day of the following month
PAYE 15th day of the following month
Withholding Tax 15th day of the following month
Final Income Tax Within 6 months after the end of the year of income
Individual Provisional Income Tax Within 3 months from the start of the year of income

Note: Filing obligations and deadlines may vary. Always confirm the latest requirements with URA.
